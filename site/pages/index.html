What it does

{{video:feature-scrub|Two sculpted objects in Blender, each with its own history slider|960|720|lazy}}

A history for every object

Up to 10,000 steps per object. A step stores only the vertices that stroke moved, about 12 KB on a 100k mesh.

{{video:feature-morph|The Morph History brush painting an earlier shape back onto one area of a sculpt|960|720|lazy}}

Paint an earlier shape back

Mark a step as the morph target, then brush it back onto the nose, or one ear. The rest stays as it is.

{{video:feature-movie|Movie Mode playing a sculpt history on the Blender timeline|960|720|lazy}}

Your sculpt as a timelapse

Movie Mode plays every step on the timeline, so you can render the whole session as a video.

Where to find it

Press N in the 3D Viewport and open the Undo History tab. The slider at the bottom of the viewport does the same job without the sidebar.

Install

  1. Download hindsight.zip. Don't unzip it.
  2. In Blender, open Edit > Preferences > Get Extensions, click the arrow at the top right and choose Install from Disk.
  3. Pick the zip. Then press N in the 3D Viewport and open the Undo History tab.
